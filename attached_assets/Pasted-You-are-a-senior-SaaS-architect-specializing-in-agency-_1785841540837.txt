## 2. Improve Agency Entity Relationships

Ensure these relationships exist:

Client:
- Has multiple projects.
- Has invoices.
- Has documents.
- Has meetings.
- Has activity history.
- Has financial summary.

Project:
- Belongs to one client.
- Contains tasks.
- Contains milestones.
- Contains deliverables.
- Contains documents.
- Tracks progress and health.

Task:
- Belongs to project.
- Has priority.
- Has status.
- Has deadline.
- Has assignee.

Deliverable:
- Belongs to project.
- Has status:
  - Draft
  - Internal Review
  - Sent to Client
  - Approved
  - Revision Requested

Invoice:
- Belongs to client/project.
- Tracks payment status.
