You are a senior AI product engineer specializing in AI assistants for business applications.

Your task is to build the **Clinic AI Assistant layer** for the Clinic / Healthcare Workspace.

The goal is to transform the clinic workspace from a simple management system into an intelligent assistant that understands the clinic's operations.

This is an MVP.

Focus on:

* Useful AI features
* Real business value
* Simple implementation
* Strong demo experience

Do not build unnecessary AI complexity.

---

# AI Assistant Identity

The AI should behave as:

"An intelligent clinic operations assistant."

It is not a generic chatbot.

It understands:

* Patients
* Appointments
* Treatments
* Follow-ups
* Billing
* Tasks
* Calendar
* Clinic activity

The AI should answer using clinic terminology.

---

# AI Context

Create a clinic-specific AI context system.

Before answering, the AI should understand:

Current workspace:

* Clinic name
* Industry type
* Current date

Clinic data:

* Number of patients
* Upcoming appointments
* Recent treatments
* Pending follow-ups
* Outstanding payments
* Tasks

---

# AI Questions

The user should be able to ask:

## Patient Management

Examples:

"How many patients do we have?"

"Show patients who need follow-up."

"Which patients haven't visited recently?"

"Summarize this patient's history."

---

## Appointments

Examples:

"What appointments do I have today?"

"Who is scheduled tomorrow?"

"Show cancelled appointments."

"Which appointments were missed?"

---

## Treatments

Examples:

"Show recent treatments."

"Which treatments are currently active?"

"Summarize this patient's treatment history."

---

## Billing

Examples:

"How much revenue did we make this month?"

"Show unpaid bills."

"Which patients have pending payments?"

---

## Tasks

Examples:

"What should I do today?"

"Show overdue clinic tasks."

"Create a follow-up task for this patient."

---

# AI Actions

The AI should not only answer.

Allow simple actions:

Create a task.

Create a follow-up.

Create an appointment.

Update task status.

Generate summaries.

Generate reports.

Example:

User:

"Create a follow-up for Ahmed next Monday."

AI:

Creates the follow-up automatically.

Add an AI section to the clinic dashboard.

---

# AI Response Style

The AI should be:

* Clear
* Short
* Action-oriented
* Professional

Avoid long generic explanations.

Focus on helping the clinic owner save time.

---

# Data Safety

The AI must only use data from the current clinic workspace.

Do not expose information outside the user's workspace.

Do not invent medical information.

Only summarize stored data.

---

# Technical Requirements

Create a reusable AI service architecture.

Avoid hardcoding clinic logic everywhere.

The system should allow future industries to have their own AI context.

Example:

Clinic:
"You are a clinic operations assistant."

Agency:
"You are an agency operations assistant."

---

# MVP Scope

Do NOT build:

* Medical diagnosis
* Treatment recommendations
* Advanced healthcare automation
* External medical integrations

Focus only on clinic management intelligence.

---

# Final Validation

Verify:

* AI understands clinic entities.
* AI answers using patient/appointment terminology.
* AI can read workspace data.
* AI can perform simple actions.
* AI summaries are useful.
* AI does not behave like a generic chatbot.

The final result should feel like a clinic manager assistant inside the application.
